## Brand & Style

This design system draws direct inspiration from award-winning Korean academic and EdTech portals—blending the intellectual authority of institution-scale research hubs (UNIST) with the fluid, friendly clarity of next-generation digital learning solutions (Codle). 

The visual personality rests on three core tenets:
- **Academic Rigor & Trust**: Crystal-clear content hierarchy, authoritative deep-slate typography, and deliberate information architecture.
- **Dynamic Clarity**: Generous atmospheric whitespace, breezy cool-slate alternating sections, and bold royal-blue focal points that command immediate visual conviction.
- **Effortless Precision**: Oversized structural indexes (01–05), immaculate rounded surfaces, and streamlined interactive pill controls that eliminate visual noise.

The style unites **Minimalism** with subtle **Modern High-Contrast Editorialism**. Pure surfaces, clean structural rhythm, and rhythmic typography supersede ornamental clutter.

## Colors

The palette balances clinical academic clarity with energetic digital blue hues.

- **Primary (`#2563EB`)**: Royal Blue for primary interactive triggers, primary focus indicators, brand anchors, and full-bleed action callouts.
- **Secondary (`#3B82F6`)**: Bright Electric Blue for micro-highlights, inline badge fills, and gradient accents.
- **Tertiary (`#1D4ED8`)**: Deep Navy Blue for active hover states and high-authority contextual elements.
- **Neutrals**:
  - `Canvas Main`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure pristine white)
  - `Canvas Alternate / Ice Slate`: `#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9` (Subtle cool backdrop for alternating modular sections)
  - `Text Headings`: `#0F172A` (Slate 900, commanding contrast)
  - `Text Body`: `#334155` (Slate 700, optimized for extended Korean and Latin reading)
  - `Text Muted`: `#64748B` (Slate 500, supplementary metadata and captions)
  - `Borders & Rules`: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200, ultra-subtle division lines)
  - `Watermark Accent`: `#DBEAFE` to `#E2E8F0` (Pale slate/blue for giant structural background numerals)

## Typography

The type scale is calibrated for simultaneous clarity in Korean Hangul and alphanumeric data. **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides geometric rhythm with humanist warmth, yielding approachable tech authority.

- **Display & Metrics**: Massive weights (`font-extrabold` / `font-black`) deliver immediate quantitative clarity on impact stats and section numerals.
- **Section Headers**: High-contrast, negative-tracking headlines paired with a selective two-tone treatment (dark slate with royal blue emphasized words).
- **Body & Captions**: Generous line heights (1.5–1.65×) guarantee comfortable readability across complex educational curricula and academic briefs.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout in this system emphasizes expansive breathing room and structured rhythm.

- **Canvas Container**: Constrained to a centered `max-w-7xl` (`1280px`) with `px-6` (`1.5rem`) on mobile/tablet and up to `px-12` on desktop viewports.
- **Vertical Pace**:
  - Major sections alternate backdrops (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8FAFC`) with vertical rhythm set to `py-20` (`5rem`) on mobile and scaling up to `py-32` (`8rem`) on desktop.
  - Component stacks preserve clean micro-hierarchies: 8px between title and metadata, 16px between body and actions, and 48px to 64px between sub-features.
- **Asymmetric Feature Rhythm (01–05 Showcase)**:
  - 12-column desktop grid split into a 5-column editorial text block alongside a 7-column media frame. Alternating or staggered rows create natural scroll pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is soft, natural, and atmospheric. Heavy borders and dark drop-shadows are avoided in favor of diffuse lighting:

- **Flat/Zero Depth**: Form controls and pill buttons sit flush with surfaces to maintain editorial cleanliness.
- **Ambient Card Elevation**: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`. Gives showcase screens and editorial cards float without visual weight.
- **Hover Transition**: Subtly shifts upward by -4px with increased diffusion (`0 20px 35px -10px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.10)`), signaling interactivity with a faint blue tint.
- **Structural Outlines**: 1px subtle borders (`#E2E8F0`) ground floating elements against pure white backgrounds.

## Shapes

The shape system leverages balanced contrasts: sharp macro layouts contrasted with friendly, hyper-rounded touch points.

- **Primary Level (`roundedness: 3`)**: Buttons, tags, and category chips use complete pill rounding (`rounded-full`), softening high-density informational density.
- **Card & Frame Surfaces**: Generous continuous curves (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` / 1.5rem to 2rem) for image viewports, hero graphic containers, and bottom action banners.
- **Visual Harmony**: The contrast between massive corner radii on frames and pure capsule-shaped interactive elements reflects the signature Codle & UNIST digital design aesthetic.

## Components

### Hero Unit
- **Structure**: Centered or left-aligned high-impact typography with bold royal-blue accent phrase.
- **Actions**: Paired pill buttons—Primary (`bg-blue-600 text-white shadow-sm hover:bg-blue-700`) and Secondary (`bg-slate-100 text-slate-800 hover:bg-slate-200`). Eliminates extraneous decorative floating badges to prioritize immediate conversion.

### 01–05 Feature Showcase Modules
- **Left Column**:
  - Oversized background numeric watermark (`01`, `02`, etc.) in `text-blue-100` or `text-slate-100` (`font-black`, `text-7xl` or `text-8xl`), positioned slightly behind or above the heading.
  - Crisp heading (`headline-lg`) with key terms highlighted in `#2563EB`.
  - Concise 2-line description (`body-md`, `#64748B`).
  - Set of pill feature tags (`bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 font-semibold px-3 py-1 text-xs rounded-full`).
  - Text link CTA with an inline right arrow (`text-blue-600 font-medium hover:underline`).
- **Right Column**:
  - 1:1 or 4:3 showcase viewport framed in `rounded-3xl` with a hairline slate border and diffuse drop shadow.

### Impact Metrics Section
- **Backdrop**: Clean white canvas with generous vertical spacing (`py-20`).
- **Metric Item**: Monolithic numeral (`text-5xl lg:text-6xl font-extrabold text-slate-900`) accompanied by an electric-blue suffix (`+` or `%`).
- **Label**: Single-line clear descriptor (`text-sm font-medium text-slate-500 tracking-normal mt-2`).

### Editorial Photo Cards (UNIST Pattern)
- **Container**: Card wrapped in `rounded-2xl bg-white border border-slate-200/80 overflow-hidden group`.
- **Image**: Fixed 16:10 or 4:3 aspect ratio, subtle scale-up animation on card hover (`group-hover:scale-105 duration-300`).
- **Meta Row**: Pill category badge (`bg-blue-50 text-blue-600 text-xs px-2.5 py-1 rounded-full font-semibold`) and calendar date (`text-xs text-slate-400`).
- **Content**: 2-line clamped bold title (`text-slate-900 font-bold group-hover:text-blue-600 transition-colors`), followed by 2-line summary text.
- **Action**: Small circular arrow button (`w-8 h-8 rounded-full border border-slate-200 flex items-center justify-center text-slate-400 group-hover:bg-blue-600 group-hover:text-white group-hover:border-blue-600 transition-all`).

### Bottom Action Banner
- **Format**: Container-width or edge-to-edge block encased in `rounded-3xl bg-blue-600 text-white py-16 px-8 text-center shadow-xl`.
- **Content**: Bold display headline with subtle subtext (`text-blue-100`).
- **CTA**: Pure white pill button (`bg-white text-blue-600 font-semibold px-8 py-3.5 rounded-full hover:bg-blue-50 transition-all shadow-md`).
